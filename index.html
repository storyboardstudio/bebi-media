How it works

Four steps

  1. 01

    Listen

    What have you got coming up? We start with what's happening in your organisation, what you need to say and who has to hear it.

  2. 02

    Shape

    We turn your brief into a campaign idea, then storyboard it, so you can see the whole thing before we film.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    One well-planned filming programme that captures the hero film, the stories and the social cuts in one go.

  4. 04

    Multiply

    We edit for every channel and audience, so the campaign keeps running long after the shoot.
